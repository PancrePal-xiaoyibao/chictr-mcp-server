研究目的：

探究胰腺癌根治术后完成标准辅助治疗的患者，接受维持化疗是否能降低复发率，延长无复发生存期和总生存期，明确其生存获益。  

Objectives of Study：

To explore whether maintenance chemotherapy reduces recurrence rate and improves recurrence-free and overall survival in patients with pancreatic cancer who have completed standard adjuvant therapy after radical resection, and to confirm its survival benefit.

纳入标准：

Inclusion criteria

排除标准：

1.胰腺癌远处转移；
2.同时伴有其他部位恶性肿瘤；
3.术后标准化疗期间出现疾病进展或因化疗不耐受等原因未完成 标准辅助治疗；
4.治疗期间存在不可控基础疾病；
5.既往接受过卡培他滨或替吉奥治疗且出现严重不良反应；
6.妊娠或哺乳期妇女；
7.正在参加其他影响本研究的临床研究；

Exclusion criteria：

1.Distant metastasis of pancreatic cancer;
2.Concurrent malignancy at other sites;
3.Disease progression during postoperative standard chemotherapy or failure to complete standard adjuvant therapy due to chemotherapy intolerance or other reasons;
4.Uncontrolled comorbidities during treatment;
5.Prior treatment with capecitabine or S-1 with severe adverse reactions;
6.Pregnant or lactating women;
7.Currently participating in other clinical studies that may affect this study;

随机方法（请说明由何人用什么方法产生随机序列）：

使用独立的基于网络的系统集中进行 1：1：1 比例的随机化。全中心统一一套中央随机系统，多中心同步入组，按预设比例分配总样本量。

Randomization Procedure (please state who generates the random number sequence and by what method)：

Randomization at a 1:1:1 ratio will be performed centrally using an independent webbased system. A unified central randomization system will be adopted across all sites for simultaneous multicenter enrollment, with the total sample size allocated according to the prespecified ratio.

The way of sharing IPD”(include metadata and protocol, If use web-based public database, please provide the url)：

No individual participant data (IPD) will be shared publicly. All original research data are archived in the scientific research file repository of Fujian Medical University Union Hospital for internal inspection only.

数据采集和管理（说明：数据采集和管理由两部分组成，一为病例记录表(Case Record Form, CRF)，二为电子采集和管理系统(Electronic Data Capture, EDC)，如ResMan即为一种基于互联网的EDC：

本研究采用纸质 CRF 病例报告表联合 EDC 电子数据采集系统双重管理：受试者基线、治疗、随访、不良事件等临床信息先规范填写纸质 CRF，经课题研究者审核无误后录入医院电子数据管理系统；数据由项目负责人统一管控、定期备份存档，严格落实受试者隐私保密管理规定。

Data collection and Management (A standard data collection and management system include a CRF and an electronic data capture：

Research data are collected and managed by standardized paper-based Case Report Form (CRF) and hospital-owned Electronic Data Capture (EDC) system. All clinical data are double-checked by investigators before electronic entry, regularly backed up and archived under confidential management to protect participants’ personal information.
